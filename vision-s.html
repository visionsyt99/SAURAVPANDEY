01

The story

What began in 2023 as experimentation with web code became an idea, then a vision, then a name. Vision_S isn't tied to one industry — it isn't an AI company, a software studio, a design agency or an education platform. It's the intersection of all of them, run by one student who kept noticing the same gap: the tools that would have helped him learn faster, build better and lead more clearly simply didn't exist yet in Nepal. So he started building them himself.

Build beyond sectors. Create beyond expectations.

03

How it grew

  1. 2023

    An experiment with web code

    A side project turns into a question: what if this became something bigger?

  2. 2023

    Vision_S becomes a name

    The brand, the backronym and the mission get defined — non-profit, service-oriented, cross-disciplinary.

  3. 2024

    World of Vision_S & YouTube

    The first digital experience ships, alongside video and cinematography work under the same banner.

  4. 2025

    AURA V.2 reaches students

    A free study companion for Nepal's +2 Science students — 500+ formulas, 1,000+ MCQs — becomes Vision_S's first real product.

  5. 2025

    CEE Simulator launches

    A second product, built for medical entrance aspirants, extends Vision_S from science class into exam prep.

  6. 2026

    Still building, still non-profit

    Vision_S stays service-oriented by design — the goal is impact for Nepali students, not a business model yet.

05

Founder lessons

01

Outcomes over complexity

Users never care how complicated the technology underneath is. They care about speed, reliability, simplicity, immediate value and a frictionless experience.

02

Feedback is the roadmap

A vision provides direction, but real users determine the route. Build → Deploy → Observe → Listen → Adapt → Improve.

03

Resilience without a blueprint

Building in uncharted territory creates problems with no obvious answer. The skill is to learn quickly, execute, adapt and refine.
