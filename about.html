About

Curiosity first

Saurav Pandey is a Nepalese student, founder and creative technologist based in Kathmandu, currently pursuing +2 Science with Biology at St. Xavier's College, Maitighar. From early curiosity about technology and biological sciences to building digital products, creative projects and Vision_S, he has consistently explored the space between disciplines rather than being confined by one.

Don't just learn what already exists. Explore what could exist.
