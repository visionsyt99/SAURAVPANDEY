The timeline

Five years, six roles, one direction — click any milestone to open it up.

2021

Role

General Member

Responsibility

Community service, social-awareness initiatives, event and fundraising support.

Scale

Club-wide participation, 40+ members

What I learned

How to show up consistently for something bigger than yourself, even in a small role.

2022

Role

Secretary

Responsibility

Maintaining records, recording meetings and events, member and advisor communication.

Scale

Full club administration

What I learned

Structure and documentation are what let a team scale beyond a handful of people.

2023

Role

Vice President

Responsibility

Leads club activities and project execution, mentors junior members, represents the club at inter-school events.

Scale

Entire Interact Club, inter-school representation

What I learned

Leadership is mostly about unblocking other people, not doing everything yourself.

2023

Role

House Captain

Responsibility

Team leadership, mentoring, strategic planning, event coordination. 🥈 First Runner-Up — Inter-House Sports Competition.

Scale

900+ students across Newton House

What I learned

Motivating 900 people takes different skills than motivating 9 — you lead through systems, not just presence.

2025

Role

Class Representative

Responsibility

Student–faculty communication, coordinating class activities and meetings, mentoring juniors.

Scale

One full +2 Science section

What I learned

Being trusted by both students and faculty means learning to translate between two very different audiences.

2026

Role

Chief Class Representative

Responsibility

Leads the network of Class Representatives across Grade 12, liaison between students, faculty and administration, oversees college events and student-led programs.

Scale

1,000+ +2 students across all sections

What I learned

At this scale, leadership is a design problem — you're building systems and culture, not just showing up to meetings.
